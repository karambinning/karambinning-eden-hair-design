About the salon

A salon for every head of hair on Tower Street.

Eden Hair Design has worked the same Tower Street address in the centre of King's Lynn for years — a few minutes from the Tuesday Market Place, behind the Corn Exchange. It's a busy independent salon, not a chain.

The chairs are kept open for cut, colour and finishing, and the clientele runs the full range: classic blow dries, balayage and Matrix & Wella colour work, alongside braiding, weaves, wig installs and perms. If you have hair, there's a service here for it.
